# AI Arena: the game-feel design

Errand 1.1 "What does an agent do on its own?", errand 1.5 "Which brain does the job?" (AI
Arena has no tier 2; 1.5 is the errand with the most parts open), and errand 1.6's decide
screen. Shared rules (menu, LUMA, briefing, debrief, measuring): [README](README.md). Every word
of every screen today, verbatim: [inventory/ai-arena.md](inventory/ai-arena.md); screenshots in
`games/ai-arena/Builds/look-audit/game-feel/before/`.

## The game in one paragraph

Today the player fills in a drawer (a name field, ten colour swatches, a list field, a "Build it
from parts" mode with fifteen BUY/NEVER toggles, ten coin buttons, four tags that cycle values)
beside a picture of the agent, then watches cards of numbers. After: the player **packs the
agent** at a market table and **sends it**. Produce goes into its basket or into the "No" crate,
and each drop writes a line on the note pinned to the agent; the note can also be written by
hand, which stays the heart of the lesson. Coins go into the battery on its back. From 1.3 on,
parts are fitted to it: a lens, a notebook, a bulb on the antenna. Then it walks off. The round
is the street, the agent's thought in a bubble, the market clock running towards dinner and the
family's faces at the window. The debrief is the agent at the front door with its basket.

## Tension: dinner time

The errand's 30 turns are the market clock's hour, from 6 to 7 in the evening: the hands move
one notch a turn, on the clock tower in the world and as a small clock face (no digits) at the
top of the screen. At the house, the family waits at the lit window; an agent that comes home
shows its face there. In the last five turns the clock's face turns amber. It ends the errand as
the 30 turns do today, no more.

## How the checks are met

| Check | Met by |
|---|---|
| 1.1 Wrote a list; Said what to buy: apples; Said what to leave at the market | The note's text, typed or written by dropping produce: "Buy apples." from the basket, "Never buy …" / "Nothing else." from the No crate. The same text the list field holds today, read by the same `Verdicts`. |
| 1.1, 1.5 Came home with dinner; bought a listed item; lasted the budget | Unchanged: the round. |
| 1.5 the cost per item, the cheapest in the class | Unchanged: computed from the round; shown big on the debrief. |
| 1.6 the five choices | The note's drop place, stored as the choice buttons store it. |

The platform's report is built from the same state, so it does not change.

## Open questions

1. **Writing by dropping produce.** It writes plain sentences on the note, which the player can
   then edit. On 1.2 ("Get fruit."), that means the player can name every fruit by dropping them,
   which is exactly the lesson ("name the things"). Is that too easy, or right?
2. **Claude's thought bubble.** Showing only its first sentence, with coordinates removed, changes
   what is displayed, not what Claude wrote or what the agent reads. Agreed?
3. **The story.** Four shots instead of six beats, once, before 1.1. Or drop it and let the 1.1
   transmission carry the story?
